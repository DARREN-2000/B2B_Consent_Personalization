# Support

## Community Support

For general questions, troubleshooting, and discussions about deploying and extending ConsentHub, please use our community channels:

*   **GitHub Discussions:** Ask questions and share ideas in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/DARREN-2000/B2B_Consent_Personalization/discussions) (if enabled).
*   **Documentation:** Review the extensive documentation in the `docs/` folder or on our [Documentation Site](https://darren-2000.github.io/B2B_Consent_Personalization/).

## Issue Tracker

If you have found a bug or have a specific feature request, please use the [GitHub Issue Tracker](https://github.com/DARREN-2000/B2B_Consent_Personalization/issues).

Please ensure you search existing issues before creating a new one to avoid duplicates.

## Enterprise Support

For organizations requiring guaranteed SLAs, architecture reviews, or custom integrations, enterprise support is available. Please reach out to [enterprise@example.com](mailto:enterprise@example.com) for more details.
