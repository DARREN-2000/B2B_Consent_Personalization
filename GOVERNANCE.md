# ConsentHub Governance

ConsentHub is an open-source project driven by its community and core maintainers. This document outlines the governance model for the project.

## Roles and Responsibilities

### Users
Anyone who uses ConsentHub. Users are encouraged to participate by opening issues, providing feedback, and helping others in discussions.

### Contributors
Anyone who contributes to the project (code, documentation, design, advocacy). Contributors are expected to adhere to the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

### Maintainers
Maintainers are active and regular contributors who have write access to the repository. They are responsible for:
*   Reviewing and merging pull requests.
*   Triaging issues and guiding architectural decisions.
*   Ensuring the project adheres to its core design principles (Auditability, Isolation, Simplicity).
*   Managing releases.

New maintainers are nominated by existing maintainers and selected based on a history of high-quality contributions and community engagement.

## Decision Making

*   **Lazy Consensus:** Most decisions (e.g., minor code changes, bug fixes) are made via lazy consensus. If a PR is open for review and no maintainer objects within a reasonable timeframe (typically 72 hours), it can be merged.
*   **Significant Changes:** Major architectural changes, API deprecations, or significant new features require a formal proposal (usually an Issue or a PR) and explicit approval from at least two maintainers.
*   **Conflict Resolution:** In the rare event of a disagreement that cannot be resolved through discussion, the maintainer team will vote, with a simple majority deciding the outcome.

## Roadmap Planning

The roadmap is maintained collaboratively. The core team reviews feature requests and community feedback periodically to update the [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).
